Biography

I build things that have to meet the real world and be right about it: a public strike record that reads the news every morning and serves an API, a CanSat payload and radio that flew, a Yagi-Uda antenna that is slowly becoming a radar, a chess engine that watches games and says what it thinks of every move.

Mostly Python, some C++ and C#, JavaScript when the thing has to live in a browser. Two research papers — one on neural scaling laws, one on globular cluster evaporation — and a habit of finishing the side projects: the games on this site play, the tools download, the extension grades.
